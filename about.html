Who We Are

Maluti Lesotho Tours is a small company from Butha-Buthe, started in 2024. We love Lesotho and want to show visitors its beautiful mountains, culture, and history.

Our mission is to help local people find work through tourism and to share the beauty of Lesotho with the world.

Our Story

We started with one car and one guide. Today, we have five guides and work with hotels and restaurants throughout Lesotho. We have helped more than 200 visitors explore our country.

Our Values

  • Respect Basotho culture
  • Help keep the environment clean
  • Support local communities
  • Provide safe, welcoming tours
